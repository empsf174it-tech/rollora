Avoid rolling directly on joints, bones, the lower back's spine, or areas of acute injury, swelling, or bruising. Avoid sharp, shooting, or numbing pain. Roll slowly with moderate pressure. Consult a qualified professional for pain, injury, or medical conditions.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I choose the right density?

Beginners should start with a soft or medium density roller. Firm and extra-firm rollers are best for experienced users needing deeper pressure.

How often should I roll?

You can roll daily. Even 5-10 minutes a day is sufficient for general maintenance and warm-ups.
